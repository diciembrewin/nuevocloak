🌿 What Is Wood?

Wood is a hard, fibrous, organic material that forms the trunk of trees. It develops through concentric layers called growth rings.

  • How it forms: Through cell division in the cambium layer, producing xylem (heartwood and sapwood).
  • Where it comes from: Natural forests and responsibly managed plantations.
  • Why it matters: Renewable, stores CO₂, and is essential in construction, furniture, art, and more.

🪵 Types of Wood

Oak

  • Origin: Europe, North America
  • Color: Honey to brown
  • Hardness: High
  • Texture: Coarse, porous
  • Uses: Furniture, barrels, flooring
  • Fun fact: Can live over 1,000 years.

Pine

  • Origin: Northern Hemisphere
  • Color: Light yellow
  • Hardness: Medium-low
  • Texture: Fine, straight
  • Uses: Construction, budget furniture
  • Fun fact: Most widely used wood worldwide.

Cedar

  • Origin: Americas, Mediterranean
  • Color: Reddish brown
  • Hardness: Medium
  • Texture: Fine, aromatic
  • Uses: Cabinetry, cladding
  • Fun fact: Its scent repels moths.

Walnut

  • Origin: Europe, Americas
  • Color: Chocolate brown
  • Hardness: High
  • Texture: Wavy, attractive
  • Uses: Luxury furniture, gunstocks
  • Fun fact: Highly prized in the 18th century.

Mahogany

  • Origin: Central & South America
  • Color: Deep red
  • Hardness: Medium
  • Texture: Fine, uniform
  • Uses: Fine furniture, instruments
  • Fun fact: National wood of Belize.

Teak

  • Origin: Southeast Asia
  • Color: Golden to brown
  • Hardness: High
  • Texture: Coarse, oily
  • Uses: Outdoor furniture, boats
  • Fun fact: Resists water and termites.

Fir

  • Origin: Cold northern regions
  • Color: White-yellowish
  • Hardness: Low
  • Texture: Fine, straight
  • Uses: Paper, light structures
  • Fun fact: The classic Christmas tree.

Eucalyptus

  • Origin: Australia
  • Color: Pinkish brown
  • Hardness: High
  • Texture: Medium
  • Uses: Posts, flooring, pulp
  • Fun fact: Grows very fast (up to 10 m/year).

Bamboo

  • Origin: Asia, Americas
  • Color: Green to brown
  • Hardness: High (at nodes)
  • Texture: Smooth, cylindrical
  • Uses: Furniture, scaffolding, textiles
  • Fun fact: Not a tree — it's a grass.

Ash

  • Origin: Europe, North America
  • Color: Creamy white
  • Hardness: High
  • Texture: Coarse, straight
  • Uses: Tool handles, furniture
  • Fun fact: Very elastic — great for sports.

🧴 Wood Care

  • Avoid excess moisture: Wood swells and warps.
  • Clean properly: Use a dry or slightly damp cloth — never soak.
  • Sun protection: UV rays fade and crack the surface.
  • Preventive maintenance: Apply natural oil or wax periodically.
  • Recommended products: Always choose natural waxes and oils (no specific brands).

♻️ Sustainability

  • Reforestation: Plant more trees than are harvested.
  • Certified forests: Look for FSC or PEFC labels.
  • Responsible harvesting: Use selective cutting techniques.
  • Environmental protection: Wood stores CO₂ throughout its life.
  • Circular economy: Reuse and recycle wood at the end of its life.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Hardwood vs. Softwood?

It's botanical: conifers are softwoods, broadleaf trees are hardwoods — not about physical hardness.

How long does wood last?

Depends on species and care. Decades indoors, or centuries in ancient structures.

Best wood for humidity?

Teak, ipe, and cumaru excel in outdoor, humid environments.

How to identify a species?

By color, grain, odor, weight, and microscopically in labs.
